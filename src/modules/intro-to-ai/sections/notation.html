Reading the Notation

every symbol in the module, said out loud
What this page is for
Most formulas in this module are short sentences written in shorthand. If you cannot read the shorthand, the sentence looks like noise — and no amount of staring fixes that, because the problem is vocabulary, not intelligence.

This page lists every symbol used anywhere in the module, what to say when you see it, and what it does. Then it takes the five worst formulas and decodes them word by word.

Three rules that make any formula readable

Do these in order and most formulas fall apart
  1. Read the left-hand side first. It tells you what is being defined and what it depends on. dist(C₁,C₂) is "a distance between two clusters" — so the answer will be one number, and it depends on two clusters.
  2. Find the little writing under Σ, min or max. That tells you what you are looping over. Under Σ it means "add one of these for every …". Under min it means "try all of these and keep the smallest".
  3. Work from the inside out. Do the innermost bracket first, exactly like arithmetic. The outer symbols just say what to do with the pile of numbers you produced.

A formula is never asking you to do something clever in one step. It is a recipe with the steps stacked up.

Choosing: min, max, arg

symbolsaygives youexample
min"the minimum of"the smallest valuemin of {3, 7, 5} = 3
max"the maximum of"the largest valuemax of {3, 7, 5} = 7
arg min"arg min"the thing that gave the smallest valuearg min = the first item
arg max"arg max"the thing that gave the largest valuearg max = the second item

min/max give a number. arg min/arg max give a name. That is the entire difference, and it is worth marks in Topics 4, 7 and 8.

If a question says "which action?" or "which feature?", it wants an arg. If it says "what is the value?", it wants the plain min or max.

The five worst formulas, decoded

1 · Single-linkage  (Topic 06)
distSL(C₁, C₂) := minp∈C₁, q∈C₂ ‖p − q‖
distSL(C₁,C₂)the single-linkage distance between two clusters — the answer will be one number
:=is defined as
mintake the smallest of everything that follows
p∈C₁, q∈C₂for every point p in cluster 1, paired with every point q in cluster 2
‖p − q‖the distance between that pair

Out loud: "measure every point of C₁ against every point of C₂, and keep the smallest distance."

2 · Within-cluster variance  (Topic 06)
V(Ck) = (1 / |Ck|)  Σi,i′ ∈ Ck  d(pi, pi′)²
V(Ck)the variance of cluster k — one number per cluster
|Ck|how many points are in it (not pairs)
Σi,i′one term for every unordered pair of points in the cluster
d(pi, pi′)²the distance between that pair, squared — no square root

Out loud: "take every pair of points in the cluster, square the distance between them, add all of those up, then divide by the number of points."

3 · The Bellman update  (Topic 04)
Ui+1(s) = R(s) + γ  maxa  Σs′ P(s′ | s, a) Ui(s′)
Ui+1(s)the new value of square s, after this sweep
R(s)the reward for standing on s — the −0.04 fee
γthe discount factor, 1 on this grid
maxatry every action a, keep the best score
Σs′one term for every square you might land on
P(s′|s,a)the chance of landing on s′ — 0.8, 0.1 or 0.1
Ui(s′)what that square was worth last sweep

Out loud: "for each action, average the old values of the squares it might take you to; keep the best action's number; discount it; add the fee for being here."

4 · Upper Confidence Bound  (Topic 08)
At = arg maxa [ Qt(a) + c √( ln t / Nt(a) ) ]
Atthe arm you pull at step t — the answer is an arm, because it is arg max
Qt(a)your current estimate of arm a
cthe exploration parameter you chose in advance
ln tnatural log of the current step number
Nt(a)how many times you have pulled a before now

Out loud: "for each arm, add its estimate to a bonus that grows when you have not tried it much; pull whichever arm scores highest."

5 · Gini-Split  (Topic 07)
Gini-Split(Ai) = Σj=1a (nj / n) · Gini(vj)
Aithe feature you are considering splitting on
Σj=1aone term for each value that feature can take — so one per child node
nj / nwhat fraction of the rows land in child j — the weight
Gini(vj)how impure that child is

Out loud: "split the rows by this feature; for each group, work out how mixed it is; average those, weighting each by how big the group is."

If a formula still will not open
Put numbers in it. Take the smallest possible example — two points, two arms, one sweep — and write out every term longhand. A formula that looks impossible in symbols is nearly always three multiplications and an addition once you have actual numbers in front of you.

Every tool in this file exists to do exactly that: they print the longhand version of the formula for whatever numbers you give them.
