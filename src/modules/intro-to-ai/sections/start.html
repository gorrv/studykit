how these notes are put together
What this file is
A single self-contained page. Everything lives here — no internet needed once it's open, nothing to install, and you can email it to yourself or print it. Each topic becomes its own section in the sidebar on the left.

What goes in as content arrives

The material, rewritten

Reorganised so it reads as an explanation rather than a list of bullets. Definitions kept verbatim where they're the sort of thing an exam asks you to state.

Interactive tools

Working implementations of whatever the topic's mechanic is — search algorithms you can step through, tables you can fill in, solvers that show their working. Built so you can check your own hand-worked answers.

Exam takeaways

A numbered list closing each topic: the things most likely to be asked, and the exact phrasings worth memorising.

Practice with hidden solutions

Past-exam questions where the answer is folded away until you've had a go, plus a self-test generator that marks you.

Conventions used throughout

A yellow box like this
Something worth learning close to word-for-word — a definition, a rule, or a list an exam expects back.
A green box
A connection, an intuition, or the reason something is defined the way it is. Understanding rather than memorising.
An orange box
A trap. Somewhere the obvious reading is wrong, or a place marks are commonly lost.
📘 A blue box marked with a book
Background beyond the core material. Useful for understanding, but check whether your exam expects it before spending time on it.
