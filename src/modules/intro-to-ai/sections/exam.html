What this is
A full paper drawn from every question generator in the file — tick the topics you want, choose a length, optionally put a clock on it. Nothing is marked until you press finish, so you can move back and forth like a real paper.

At the end you get a per-topic breakdown, every question you got wrong with its full explanation, and a retry-the-wrong-ones button. Numbers are randomised on every draw, so re-sitting the same selection is a genuinely new paper.

Exam mode

Sit a paper

How to use it in the last few days
  1. All topics, 40 questions, untimed. This is a diagnostic, not a test — you are looking for which rows come back red.
  2. Re-sit with only the red topics ticked, 20 questions, until they are green. Read the explanation on every wrong answer before moving on.
  3. Then all topics again, 60 questions, on a 90-minute clock. The point of the clock is to find out which topics you can do but cannot do quickly — those are the ones that cost you marks under pressure.

A score below 50% on a topic means go back to that topic's section. Between 50% and 70% usually means the theory page rather than the worked examples — it is normally a definition or a distinction that is missing, not the method.

Good luck.
