Why these are worth more than the mock exam
The mock exam draws from a fixed bank, so once you have seen a question you half-remember the answer. These are generated: the processes, addresses, reference strings and allocation matrices are different every time, and your answer is checked by running the same simulator the tools use. You cannot pass by recognising the question — only by doing the working.

If you get one wrong
Every explanation shows the working, not just the answer — the Gantt trace for a scheduling question, the hit/miss sequence for a replacement one, the safety walk for Banker's. Read it, then draw the next one on the same topic and do it by hand before pressing Check.
